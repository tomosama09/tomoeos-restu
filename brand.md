# Brand Doc — tomoeOS

## Nama

**Frutiger Aero Studio OS** (nama sistem di layar: "tomoeOS").

## Palat (palet warna)

- Aero glass: linear-gradient kebiruan transparan (`rgba(230,245,255,0.68)` → `rgba(175,215,255,0.48)`), blur 30px + saturate 210%.
- Aksen: `#0099ff` / `#0055cc` (biru Windows 7).
- Teks OS: `#0c2340` (biru gelap), label sekunder `#1e6fb8`.
- Tombol jelly: white→blue gradient.

## Font

**Segoe UI** → `Tahoma, Arial, sans-serif` (fallback). Monospaced: `Consolas`.

## Icon

Ikon Win7 autentik (`public/icons/*.webp`) + SVG Iconify open-license. Gaya glassmorphism + drop-shadow.

## Gaya bahasa

Bahasa Inggris, tone storytelling dan kredibel (bukan AI-slop). Tanpa em dash, tanpa bintang rating, tanpa jargon kosong. Angka spesifik (440,000+ views, 53.8%).

## Contoh copy (yang sudah final)

> "In 2023 the campus library handed me its Instagram and TikTok with no playbook. I grew those channels past 440,000 organic views, and more than half the audience had never followed us."
