Restu Utomo Multimedia Production Help & Guide Center

Q: How does the ADDIE-based production workflow run?

Every project starts with Analysis (material goals & audience), Design (storyboard & script), Development (shooting & DaVinci Resolve post-production), Implementation (standard-format distribution), and Evaluation (audience retention audit).

Q: What is the average turnaround time?

For trailers or aftermovies (1–3 minutes): 3–5 working days. For e-learning tutorial series (10–15 scenes): 2–3 structured working weeks.

Q: How do revisions work?

Every contract includes 2 structured revision rounds (cut adjustments, color correction, text timing, or audio leveling) per the agreed storyboard.

Q: Are you available for on-site field coverage?

Yes. I live in Sleman, D.I. Yogyakarta, and I cover field activities and weekend agendas.

Q: Are you available for training or workshops?

Yes. I hold a 2026 Trainer certification in AI-Based Information Literacy. I taught social media strategy to KKN students and write research guides on Mendeley, VOSviewer, Publish or Perish, AI ethics, and h-index analysis.
